How to use · कैसे इस्तेमाल करें

  1. Bachhe ka naam English ya Hindi me likho — sheet turant ban jayegi.
  2. Pehli row me solid naam dekho, phir dotted naam ko pencil se trace karo — har row me dots halke hote jayenge.
  3. Aakhri blank rows par bachha khud, bina dekhe, naam likhne ki koshish karega.
  4. Teacher ho? “Class list” mode me saare naam ek-ek line me daalo — poori class ki sheets ek PDF me mil jayengi.
